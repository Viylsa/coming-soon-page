FAQ

Questions from local venues.

Do you only cover Islamabad and Rawalpindi?+

The twin cities and the Potohar region around them are where we work fastest and where there is no travel cost to pass on. We do shoot elsewhere in Pakistan — tell us where the venue is and we will quote the travel honestly rather than pretend distance is free.

How long does a shoot take?+

Most venues are captured in one afternoon. You do not need to close: we work around your opening hours and shoot only the spaces you have chosen. NUTECH's fourteen scenes were a single visit.

Does the AI guide really answer in Urdu?+

Yes, and a visitor can switch language mid-conversation. It answers only from the documents you approve, so it will say it does not know rather than invent a fee or a deadline. There is a working demo on our homepage.

What does it cost?

Every venue is a different size, so each package is quoted to your space. One call to scope it, then a fixed price in writing before anyone turns up with a camera — never a surprise at the end. See what each package includes →

Who owns the tour and the photographs?+

You do. The panoramas are of your building, and the visitor data the tour collects is yours. Hosting is included for the period in your package, and you can take the whole thing elsewhere afterwards.
